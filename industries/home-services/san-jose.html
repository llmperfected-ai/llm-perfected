Local questions we hear often

San Jose is a big, spread-out service area — can the AI route calls by neighborhood or job zone?

Yes. Call flows can be configured to tag calls by ZIP or service zone so dispatch knows which part of the metro a caller is in before a tech is assigned.

Can this cover both English and Spanish-speaking callers in San Jose, along with the area's other common languages?

English and Spanish are supported out of the box, with the system detecting which language a caller is using and responding in kind. San Jose's ACS language data (58.9% speaking a language other than English at home) reflects a broader mix than just English and Spanish — reach out if your call volume justifies configuring an additional language.

Can this handle emergency after-hours calls for HVAC or plumbing?

Yes. After-hours calls can be answered, triaged, and escalated to an on-call tech based on the rules you set.
