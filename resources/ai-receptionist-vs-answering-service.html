Where each option tends to make sense

A self-serve AI app can work well for a very small, simple business willing to build and maintain its own scripts. A human answering service still makes sense for businesses that need judgment calls a script can't anticipate on every single call. A configured service like LLM Perfected is built for businesses that want the coverage and bilingual reach of AI without doing the configuration and maintenance themselves.
