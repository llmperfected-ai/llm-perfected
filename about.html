Built for the way Bay Area small businesses actually run

LLM Perfected builds AI-powered front-office systems — call answering, scheduling, lead follow-up, and the paperwork around a job — for small businesses across the San Francisco Bay Area.

What we actually do

We configure an AI receptionist and the automation around it specifically for each client: their services, their pricing, their calendar, their escalation rules. It's not a one-size-fits-all chatbot — every client goes through a setup process before anything goes live to real callers.

Why the Bay Area specifically

Home values, labor costs, and call volume patterns here don't look like the national average, and a large share of callers in this region speak Spanish as a first or preferred language — see the language data behind that. We built the service around those specifics rather than importing a generic national product.
