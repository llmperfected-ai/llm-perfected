FAQ

Frequently asked questions about our AI receptionist

What is an AI receptionist?

An AI receptionist is software that answers incoming business calls in place of (or alongside) a human front desk — picking up, understanding what the caller needs, booking appointments directly onto a calendar, answering routine questions, and escalating anything it can't handle to a real person. LLM Perfected configures one specifically around a client's own services, pricing, and scheduling rules rather than running a generic script.

How much does an AI receptionist cost?

At LLM Perfected, flat monthly pricing runs $347–$697/mo depending on plan, plus a one-time setup fee ($900–$1,800), with custom pricing for multi-location or high-volume teams. That's flat-rate, not the per-minute billing common with traditional human answering services. See the full plan breakdown in the pricing section above.

How fast can LLM Perfected be live for my business?

Most Bay Area clients are live within 5–7 business days of signing. We script your call flows, connect your calendar and CRM, and run a review call with you before anything goes live to real customers.

What happens when the AI can't handle a call?

Every call flow includes escalation rules you set. Calls that need a human — a complaint, a complex quote, anything outside the script — are routed to your cell, a teammate, or a message queue, never left hanging.

Does it work in Spanish as well as English?

Yes. English and Spanish are supported out of the box and the system can detect which language a caller is using and respond in kind, without a separate phone line.

Is this the same as a generic chatbot or voice AI app?

No. Off-the-shelf AI apps are built for the average user. We configure your scripts, booking rules, and escalation paths specifically for your business before launch, and keep tuning them as your business changes.

Will customers know they're talking to an AI?

We disclose it when asked or when required, and we design conversations to be direct and useful rather than to disguise what it is. The goal is a fast, accurate answer, not a trick.

What does setup actually involve on my end?

A 30–45 minute kickoff call to map your services, pricing, and calendar rules, plus access to the scheduling tool you already use. We build the call flows; you review and approve them before launch.

Do you build AI agents for things other than answering the phone?

Yes. The AI receptionist is our most requested agent, but we also build agents for sales qualification, outbound follow-up, scheduling and dispatch, document and proposal drafting, recruiting, reputation management, and recurring research and reporting. We scope which ones actually make sense for your business before recommending any of them.
